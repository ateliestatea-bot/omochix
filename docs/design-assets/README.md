# OmochiX visual concept v1

Built-in image generation was used to create five original, text-free editorial assets. These assets are inserted into `../omochix-design-comp.html`, a visual concept using the existing homepage layout and local test content. They are not assigned to production WordPress articles.

Palette and direction: violet, chrome, ivory; realistic materials, sculptural editorial compositions, generous negative space. No mascot appears in these assets. The existing mascot remains in the hero, and the footer stays image-free.

Prompt set:

- Feature: Premium editorial website cover artwork for OmochiX AI media. A sculptural translucent violet glass ribbon forming an elegant infinite loop above a matte black architectural plinth, luminous white studio backdrop, soft lavender horizon. Refined museum art direction, realistic materials and subtle reflections, violet black silver white palette. Wide landscape, no logos, lettering, mascots or people.
- News: Macro photograph of brushed silver computational chip architecture with subtle violet luminous paths, diagonal metallic layers, pale gray studio, contemporary design magazine aesthetic. Realistic physical materials. Wide landscape, no text, logos, mascots or people.
- Prompts: Floating folded sheet of translucent violet glass and a small polished silver sphere, sculptural paper-like folds curling gracefully, cream gallery backdrop, tactile 3D editorial art, soft daylight. Wide landscape, no text, logos, mascots or people.
- Learn: Three ascending steps in frosted lilac glass, polished chrome sphere on the upper step, airy off-white studio background, natural light and gentle shadows, learning and progress metaphor. Wide landscape, no text, logos, mascots or people.
- Tools: Rounded chrome cube, translucent violet prism and matte charcoal sphere arranged on ivory studio ground, product photography and contemporary sculpture, subtle lavender reflections and generous negative space. Wide landscape, no text, logos, mascots or people.

The final assets are `feature-v1.webp`, `news-v1.webp`, `prompts-v1.webp`, `learn-v1.webp`, and `tools-v1.webp` in this folder. The standalone HTML embeds all assets and can be opened offline.

QA: 320, 390, 768 and 1440px × light/dark. All images loaded, no horizontal overflow. `comp-*` images show local test content and conceptual visuals, not published news.
